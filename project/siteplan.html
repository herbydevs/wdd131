Website Planning Document

Peak & Pine Adventure Guide

Site Name

Name: Peak & Pine Adventure Guide

Reason: I chose this name because it evokes the imagery of mountain peaks and coniferous forests, which are the primary focus of the national park hiking guides provided on the site. It sounds professional yet adventurous, targeting outdoor enthusiasts.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Peak & Pine is to provide a curated hub for high-quality, reliable hiking trail information and seasonal preparation guides for National Parks in the Pacific Northwest. The site will offer detailed trail maps, difficulty ratings, and a packing list generator to ensure hikers are safe and prepared.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "Which trails in Olympic National Park are best for a beginner hiker during the rainy spring season?"
  • Scenario 2: "What specific gear is required for an overnight backpacking trip in the North Cascades compared to a day hike?"

Color Schema

The site uses a nature-inspired palette to connect users to the outdoors. This document applies the schema below.

Primary: #2D5A27
(Header, Navigation, Headings)

Secondary: #F4F1DE
(Page Background)

Accent: #E07A5F
(Buttons, Highlights, Call-to-action)

Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat (Bold) - Used for all headers and navigation links to provide a modern, high-visibility look.

Body Font: Open Sans - Used for all paragraphs, lists, and form inputs for its excellent readability on both desktop and mobile screens.
